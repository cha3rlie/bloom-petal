Questions, answered

How fast is delivery?

We deliver across Lagos the same day for orders placed before 3pm. Later orders go out the next morning.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. Add a note in your cart, or message us on WhatsApp for a custom arrangement.

How do I make my flowers last longer?

Trim the stems at an angle, use clean water with the flower food provided, and keep them away from direct sun.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

At the moment we deliver within Lagos. Message us for other cities and we will do our best.

How do I pay?

After you send your order on WhatsApp, we confirm the total and share our payment details.

Need something custom?

Events, weddings, corporate arrangements — tell us your colours and budget.
